How LeadPilot AI works

Set it up in an afternoon. Your team starts with a list of warm leads the next morning.

  1. Connect your leads

    Upload a spreadsheet or connect your forms and ads. LeadPilot cleans and verifies the numbers.

  2. Launch a campaign

    Pick WhatsApp, AI voice calls or both, write your message, and press start.

  3. Follow up on interest

    See who replied or showed interest, and get reminders for who your team should call next.
